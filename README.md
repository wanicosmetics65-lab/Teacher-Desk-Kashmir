🔑 How to Access TeacherDesk Kashmir

Before using TeacherDesk Kashmir, every new user must sign up and create their login credentials. Once registration is complete, they can log in to access the application.

📝 Step 1 — Visit the Website

Open the official TeacherDesk Kashmir website using your browser.

<div align="center">🌐 "teacherdeskkashmir.edgeone.dev" (https://teacherdeskkashmir.edgeone.dev/)

</div>👤 Step 2 — Sign Up

New users must register for an account before accessing the application.

- Open the Sign Up page.
- Enter the information requested by the application.
- Provide your institute details where required.
- Follow the on-screen registration instructions.

🔐 Step 3 — Create Your Login Credentials

Complete the registration process and create your login credentials, including your login key and password, as required by the application.

«🔒 Security tip: Keep your login key and password private. Never share your password with anyone.»

🔓 Step 4 — Log In to Your Account

After successfully completing registration:

1. Open the login page.
2. Enter your login key.
3. Enter your password.
4. Submit your credentials to log in.

Once your credentials are verified, you can access your account.

🎯 Step 5 — Start Using TeacherDesk Kashmir

After logging in, explore the tools available to your account.

Account Role| What You Can Do
🧑‍🏫 Teacher| Manage classes, students, attendance, assessments, grades, lesson plans and other classroom activities.
🏫 Head of Institute| Access additional institute-level management features, review teacher information and publish announcements.

Available features depend on your account role and configured permissions.
